Communication Reflection

In this section, I will reflect on how I have grown as a speaker and presenter through my Spring 2025 semester.

As a computer science major, I need to present my work and answer questions concisely and easily. Thankfully, through my time at UTD, I have been able to participate in multiple hackathons. In these hackathons, I presented to a series of different judges ranging from professors to industry professionals. Although these experiences have given me a deeper understanding of how to sell an idea, they took place after hours of tireless work, preventing me from preparing a well-rounded presentation.

To combat this, classes such as software engineering and business communication allowed me to truly take my time developing a presentation that enabled me to share my ideas and research. These opportunities have given me the experience required to convey myself in a more professional and confident way.

Presentation Reflection

For our business communication presentation on self-awareness, our group did a fantastic job presenting our ideas through our slide presentation. Before our presentation, we had a few moments to finalize our thoughts and practice our speaking points. Unfortunately, we had forgotten to bring a rubric used for grading so we were deducted points.

From this experience, we learned always to be prepared for anything. For future presentations, I will be sure to have any material I may need to get my point across correctly.
