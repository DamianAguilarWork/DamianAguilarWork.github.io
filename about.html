Writing Reflection

In this section, I will share a few documents I have worked on in my academic career. More importantly, I will demonstrate how I have learned to write professionally.

Before coming to college, I had learned much about writing through participating in AP Language and Composition and AP Literature and Composition. These classes discussed many ways of writing expository and argumentative essays. This allowed me to comprehend different writing styles better and gave me a deep understanding of conveying my thoughts through writing.

Although this was important for my growth in writing and persuasion, I had much to learn when it came to writing technical and professional documents. Thankfully, as I have continued my education, I have developed my writing abilities, not just in business communication, but in technical writing as well. I intend to continue my writing growth as I pursue my business administration minor and computer science major.
